## Standards And Official Sources

The Standards and Authoritative Sources stream is maintained separately from
scientific and coding-agent-specific evidence. Its initial public-safe
inventory is [`authoritative-sources.md`](./authoritative-sources.md).

Initial source classes are:

- normative standards: ISO/IEC/IEEE 29148:2018 and ISO/IEC 25010:2023;
- body of knowledge: SWEBOK V4.0a.

The inventory records source identity, status, version relationships, access
and copyright boundaries, provisional research-domain mappings, and evidence-
use limits. It does not treat these sources as empirical proof or as direct
coding-agent evidence. Later source extraction and transfer analysis remain
pending.
